# BeanTI

Status: approved in conversation

An illustrated coffee travel journal. Traditional Chinese, warm, curious, concise. Cute bean characters, generous ivory space, editorial typography, restrained motion.

Palette: paper #faf7ef, espresso #493b30, muted #6b5e52, sage #536647, pink #f0d9d3, yellow #f4e7b9, apricot #ecd4b8. Semantic colors and surface tokens live in app/globals.css.

Typography: rounded local Chinese sans for body, Chinese serif for expressive headings, Georgia for English editorial text. No runtime external font dependency.

Approved light-only visual identity. Images are generated using the built-in image generation tool. No telemetry, accounts, or third-party tracking.
